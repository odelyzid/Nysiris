Nysiris is a private browser and client for the Nym mixnet.

Requests made in the app travel through the mixnet, so the network sees traffic
patterns rather than who is talking to whom and when. Open private links, send
messages to a Nym address, and (when connected to a social provider you run)
follow a community timeline with end-to-end encrypted direct messages.

On Android it adds a native enhancement over the same web app: app secrets such
as your social identity are sealed with AES-256-GCM keys held in the
hardware-backed Android Keystore, so a WebView compromise or a device backup
never exposes them in plaintext.

Experimental software, not yet audited. Apache-2.0. No trackers, no ads, and no
proprietary dependencies.
